Product Overview

Doctor’s Best Magnesium Glycinate features TRAACS® chelated magnesium, a highly absorbable form bound with glycine for superior absorption and excellent stomach tolerance.

It supports muscle relaxation, nervous system function, heart health, stress reduction, and quality sleep without the laxative effect common in other magnesium forms.
